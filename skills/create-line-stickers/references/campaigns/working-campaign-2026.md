Title: Apply to join "Working People Stickers Campaign" to win special incentives!
Application Period: 9:00 am on July 14, 2026 - 11:59 pm on September 4, 2026 (GMT+7)

Banner Advertisement Period: 9:00 am on September 1, 2026 - 11:59 pm on September 15, 2026 (GMT+7)

Sticker types:Static stickers/Animated stickers/Big stickers/Pop-up stickers/Effect stickers

Thanks for using LINE Creators Market.

Between September 1, 2026 to September 15, 2026, LINE Creators Market will be hosting a new special “Working People Stickers Campaign”.

Criteria for Participation
•Creators must apply this campaign via only Creator Market website (https://creator.line.me/). Creators, however, cannot participate in this campaign via LINE Sticker Maker application.
•Creators must submit a set of stickers containing at least 8 stickers and such stickers set must have a sales price at least 35 THB. However, Creators may submit a set of stickers higher than these thresholds to Company.
•Creators must submit a set of stickers in the form of static, animated, big, popup, or effect stickers only.
•Submitted stickers must be new only (stickers already on sale cannot be updated and resubmitted for participation in this campaign).
•Particular name stickers or any stickers aiming at specific individuals that were or have already been released in Sticker Shop or LINE STORE are not accepted.
•At least half of the stickers (including main image) in the stickers set must be related to Working People Stickers Campaign, such as actions, feelings about working alone, working with colleague or Stickers related to working topic. In case that a Creator submits a sticker set containing 40 stickers, there must have stickers relating to Working People Stickers Campaign.
•Please note that if a content of stickers is irrelevant to the aforementioned requirements, such sticker set will be rejected, and a Creator will be unable to participate in this campaign.
