# LINE static sticker specification

Source checked August 14, 2026: [LINE Creators Market Creation Guidelines](https://creator.line.me/en/guideline/sticker/). Re-check the live page before final submission because platform requirements can change.

## Required images

| Asset | Quantity | Pixel size |
|---|---:|---:|
| Main image | 1 | exactly 240 × 240 |
| Sticker images | 8, 16, 24, 32, or 40 | no more than 370 × 320 |
| Chat thumbnail icon | 1 | exactly 96 × 74 |

All images must:

- Be PNG files in RGB color mode with transparent backgrounds.
- Have even-numbered width and height; LINE resizes sticker images automatically.
- Use at least 72 dpi.
- Be no larger than 1 MB each.
- Leave roughly 10 px between trimmed artwork and surrounding content.

A single ZIP upload must be no larger than 60 MB.

## Metadata limits

| Field | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Creator | 50 characters |
| Sticker title | 40 characters |
| Sticker description | 160 characters |
| Copyright | 50 characters |

Asian-language characters and some symbols count as two characters each. Confirm borderline metadata in the LINE submission form.

## Design guidance

Prefer stickers that work naturally in daily conversation and communicate through immediately understandable expressions, messages, and illustrations. Maintain visibility at small size and meaningful variety across the set. Avoid object/scenery-only images, narrow or overly tall full-body compositions, pale low-contrast sets, and repetitive number/string sets.
