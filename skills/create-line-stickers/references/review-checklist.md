# LINE sticker review checklist

Source checked August 14, 2026: [LINE Creators Market Review Guidelines](https://creator.line.me/en/review_guideline/). Use the live policy as the final authority.

## Images and usefulness

- Match LINE's image format and make every design easy to use in conversation.
- Keep artwork visible and varied; avoid logo-only or text-only designs.
- Check every phrase for spelling and consistency with the title and description.
- Make `main.png` and `tab.png` representative of the actual set.
- Remove duplicates of released or currently reviewed stickers.

## Metadata and promotion

- Keep title, description, creator, and copyright within their required formats.
- Remove spelling errors, URLs, device-dependent emoji/symbols, advertising, release announcements, and search instructions.
- Avoid extremely short or misleading metadata.
- Do not promote businesses, recruiting, donations, third-party benefits, other messenger services, or requests for personal information/IDs.

## Safety and suitability

Reject concepts involving criminal promotion, graphic violence, abuse, sexual content, excessive substance use, underage alcohol/tobacco, impaired driving, realistic illegal weapons, phishing, personal data, targeted attacks, hateful or discriminatory content, political/election material, religious solicitation, gambling, self-harm, bullying, or deliberately disgusting/offensive imagery.

## Rights

- Use original material or retain documented permission for every third-party element.
- Avoid unauthorized brands, copyrighted characters, fan art with unclear rights, patents, trademarks, photos, likenesses, and caricatures.
- Obtain consent and proof for recognizable people and photos; LINE may request documentation.
- Check the laws and third-party rights in every intended sales region.

Approval remains discretionary. Passing this checklist reduces obvious rejection risks but does not promise acceptance.
