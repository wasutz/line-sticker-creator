# Thai sticker wording reference

Wording patterns that actually sell in the Thai LINE Creators Market, transcribed from live top-ranked sets. Use this when a request needs original Thai wording (style-only mode), when filling gaps in a two-reference brief, or when checking that a phrase list covers real chat jobs instead of dictionary phrases.

Checked August 15, 2026. Slang ages fast — re-run the refresh procedure at the bottom before trusting the Trend tier.

## What the ranked sets have in common

- **One phrase per sticker, 1–2 short lines.** Almost never a full sentence.
- **Mood beats politeness.** The 2025–26 winners lead with exhaustion, laziness, sarcasm, and money jokes; polite daily phrases fill the remainder.
- **Particles carry the tone.** The same word ships as `ค่ะ` / `ฮับ` / `จ้า` / `น้า` variants across sets.
- **Repeated final letters signal volume**: `หิววววว`, `กรี๊ดดดดดด`, `รวยๆๆ`, `ๆๆ`.
- **Wordless reactions are ~10–25% of a set.** Lazy Ducks is over half wordless.
- **A pack usually mixes tiers**: ~40% daily utility, ~35% mood, ~15% trend slang, ~10% wordless.

## Tier 5 — Money and luck

Popular, but stay clear of gambling.

รวยๆ · วันนี้รวย · ขอให้รวยๆ · โอนแล้วฮับ · เงินเข้าแล้ว · เงินหมดแล้ววว · สลิงแตก · ห้ามยืมเงิน · ไม่ได้รวย แค่กล้าใช้เงิน · อยากรวยแบบไม่ต้องทำงาน · ขอบคุณที่โอนนะคะ

Do not use: หวย, เลขเด็ด, บาคาร่า, or anything that reads as lottery/betting promotion — LINE review rejects gambling.

## Tier 6 — Wordless reactions

Reserve 10–25% of any set. Best performers in the ranked packs: hands-over-mouth shock, dead-flat stare, sliding off the chair, crying puddle, arms crossed sulking, thumbs up, waving goodbye, blowing a kiss, melting on the floor, sleeping under a blanket.

## Particles, spelling, and register

- **Polite female:** ค่ะ (statement) / คะ (question) — using คะ for a statement reads as a spelling error to Thai buyers. `ค่า`, `ค๊า`, `ค่าา` are the soft casual forms.
- **Polite male:** ครับ, casual ฮับ / คับ.
- **Neutral casual:** จ้า, จ้าา, น้า, นะ, เนอะ, ล่ะ.
- **Vowel stretching = loudness/whining:** หิววว, ไม่ไหวววว, รักมากกก. Two to five repeats reads natural; more looks like a typo at chat size.
- **ๆ** repeats the word: สู้ๆ, รวยๆ, ไวๆ. Common and expected.
- **Laughing** is 555 (never "haha"); 5555+ for harder laughs.
- **Mixed-script hooks** are normal and often the strongest tab image: OK, NO, Thank you, Good night, HBD.
- Avoid crude pronouns (กู / มึง) and profanity — see risks.

## Layout rules for Thai lettering

- 1 line ≈ 10–12 Thai characters at 42 px Mali Bold on a 350 px canvas; go to 36 px or split with `|` beyond that.
- Max 2 lines. 3 lines only for a stacked, deliberately handwritten look.
- Thai stacks vowels and tone marks above the line — leave ~1.3× line height so ไม้โท/ไม้ตรี never clip the sticker edge.
- Render through `scripts/render_thai_text.py` (Pango/HarfBuzz). Do not lay out Thai with ImageMagick `-annotate`; it breaks shaping.
- Dark warm-gray fill `#5b4a47` with a white keyline stays legible on both light and dark chat backgrounds. Pure black reads harsh next to pastel art.
- Key words in a second color (red/pink) is the standard emphasis trick in ranked sets: `พรุ่งนี้ทำงาน` with `ทำงาน` in red.

## Review risks in wording

- **Crude pronouns and profanity:** กู, มึง, ควาย, and censored forms. Common in edgy sets but raises rejection risk; swap to ฉัน / เรา / เค้า.
- **Self-harm reading:** ไม่อยากอยู่, อยากตาย, สู่ขิต used literally. Keep death jokes visually light or cut them.
- **Gambling, alcohol, tobacco:** lottery numbers, drinking invitations with visible alcohol.
- **Brands and IP:** no logos, no real product names, no celebrity nicknames or catchphrases owned by someone else.
- **Politics and religion:** no campaign material, no solicitation. Merit/luck phrasing (สิ่งศักดิ์สิทธิ์ๆ, สาธุ) is fine and widely used.
- **Duplication:** do not lift another creator's phrase list wholesale. Individual short slang words are shared vocabulary; a 40-slot copy of one pack's wording is not.
- **Metadata:** no URLs, no "please buy", no device-dependent emoji in title/description.

## Refresh procedure

Slang in Tier 3 should be re-verified before every new pack. From the repository root:

```bash
cd tmp && mkdir -p wording-research && cd wording-research
curl -sL -A 'Mozilla/5.0' 'https://store.line.me/stickershop/showcase/top_creators/th' -o creators.html
grep -oE 'stickershop/product/[0-9]+/th' creators.html | awk '!s[$0]++' | head -10

# per set id
id=35871153
curl -sL -A 'Mozilla/5.0' "https://store.line.me/stickershop/product/$id/th" -o p-$id.html
mkdir -p set-$id
grep -oE 'https://stickershop.line-scdn.net/stickershop/v1/sticker/[0-9]+/android/sticker.png' p-$id.html \
  | awk '!s[$0]++' | nl | while read n u; do curl -s "$u" -o "set-$id/$(printf '%02d' $n).png"; done
magick montage set-$id/*.png -thumbnail 190x190 -background '#f4f4f4' -geometry 190x190+4+4 -tile 8x sheet-$id.png
```

Then read each `sheet-<id>.png` and transcribe the phrases. A word earns Tier 3 only when it shows up in two or more currently-ranked sets. Animated-sticker pages return no `android/sticker.png` URLs — skip them.
