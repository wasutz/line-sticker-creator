# LINE Sticker Concept Research: 40-piece Thai office chat

**Researched:** 2026-08-15  
**Scope:** Character and wording  
**Locale:** Thai (Thailand)  
**Downstream skill:** create-line-stickers

## Executive Recommendation

Build an original **round office messenger pigeon** for Thai coworkers aged roughly 22–40: diligent in work threads, quietly frazzled in team chats, and warm enough for peer-to-peer encouragement. The live Thai Creators ranking contains several office/work and burnout sets, while the inspected examples lean heavily toward human office girls, generic round white figures, or text-only designs. A blue-gray pigeon creates a recognizable but less crowded silhouette and makes email, document delivery, follow-up, and “message received” actions feel native to the character. This differentiation is an inference from the inspected sample, not a claim that no similar pigeon set exists.

Use a **24 core-work / 8 mood-and-support / 4 current-slang / 4 wordless** structure. Keep all written wording on one line. The office core should remain durable; the four slang slots are deliberately a minority and should be rechecked immediately before production.

## Character Opportunity

### Audience and chat persona

- Thai office workers and hybrid/remote teams who need polite replies without sounding stiff.
- Persona: dependable junior-to-midlevel teammate; moves quickly when delivering work, freezes comically when scope changes, and decompresses after meetings.
- Relationship range: safe for coworkers and managers, warmer and more expressive with close teammates.

### Original design direction

Create a generic, wholly original **city pigeon employee**, provisionally nicknamed “น้องพิราบส่งงาน” only as an internal brief label. It has a compact pear-shaped body, small slate-blue wings, a cream belly, coral feet and beak, two short charcoal neck bars, dot eyes, and one stray crown feather shaped like a tiny paper tick. Do not model it on a known mascot, branded character, photograph, or another sticker set.

Its visual joke is “the office messenger who always delivers”: envelopes can sit under one wing, a document can be carried in the beak, and wing poses can naturally become salutes, typing, presenting, face-palming, or collapsing over a keyboard. Clothing stays minimal—an unbranded teal lanyard with a blank badge may appear only when useful—so the silhouette remains readable.

### Silhouette, palette, texture, and expression range

- **Silhouette:** round head flowing into a pear body; short triangular tail; two coral stick legs; one crown feather; wings clearly separated from the torso.
- **Palette:** slate blue `#7F9FB2`, pale cream `#FFF2D8`, charcoal `#48545A`, coral `#F28B72`, muted teal accent `#65B8AE`.
- **Line and texture:** warm charcoal line, medium weight, slightly imperfect hand-drawn contour; mostly flat color with one soft cheek blush and no photo texture.
- **Expression range:** calm acknowledgement, brisk action, proud completion, apologetic bow, attentive waiting, confused freeze, deadline panic, drained slump, supportive cheer, and silent reactions.
- **Text treatment:** dark warm charcoal with a white keyline; one accent color for the operative word only. Keep every written phrase on one horizontal line.

### Differentiation and crowded niches

Observed crowded directions in the sample are pastel office girls, generic round white figures, rabbits, cats, bears, and text-only bubbles. The messenger pigeon is recommended as a differentiated inference because its beak, tail, two-tone wing, and delivery behavior remain identifiable at thumbnail size. Avoid turning it into another featureless white blob, dressing it as a near-copy of a human-office set, or borrowing distinctive facial construction from the inspected characters.

### Props and conversation scenarios

- Envelope, document stack, paper plane, blank laptop, blank phone, calendar, clock, coffee cup without branding, headset, presentation pointer, office chair, loading bar, and tiny notification marks.
- Core scenarios: acknowledge; accept task; ask for details; send mail/file/summary; follow up; join/finish meeting; update status; finish work; wait; apologize; take a break; encourage; celebrate; panic; collapse; sign off.
- Keep devices and UI generic, with no real app logos, company marks, email addresses, URLs, or identifiable data.

### Rights and review risks

- Use only original pigeon drawings, type treatment, props, and compositions; do not trace the inspected sets or copy their complete phrase order.
- Avoid brands, real company names, recognizable people, political or religious messaging, recruitment/advertising, and promotional metadata.
- Avoid profanity/crude pronouns, bullying, gambling/lottery language, alcohol/tobacco/substance cues, weapons, self-harm readings, and literal death jokes.
- A lanyard badge must stay blank. Screens and documents must contain only abstract lines, never personal information.
- Passing this risk screen does not guarantee LINE approval; verify against the live review guidelines before submission.

## Wording Recommendation

### Register and tone

Use polite female particles (`ค่ะ` for statements, `คะ` for questions) with a friendly team-chat voice. This gives the pack one consistent identity and fits the inspected polite office segment. Stretching appears only in `เลิกงานแล้วค่า` to soften the sign-off. If a male-coded edition is later desired, rewrite the whole pack consistently rather than mixing `ค่ะ` and `ครับ`.

### Coverage targets

- **Core work/daily utility:** 24/40 (60%)
- **Mood and support:** 8/40 (20%)
- **Current slang:** 4/40 (10%)
- **Wordless reactions:** 4/40 (10%)
- **Layout invariant:** all written wording must render on one line; reduce size within the approved legibility floor instead of wrapping.

### Redundancies and phrases to avoid

- Keep `รับทราบค่ะ` (acknowledgement), `ได้เลยค่ะ` (acceptance), and `โอเคค่ะ` (confirmation): they are adjacent but perform different chat jobs. Do not add further synonyms such as `โอเครับทราบ`.
- Keep `เสร็จแล้วค่ะ` for task completion and `ตรวจเรียบร้อยค่ะ` for checking; do not add a third generic `เรียบร้อยค่ะ`.
- Avoid stale/narrow meme hooks such as `พส`, `ของแทร่`, celebrity catchphrases, and phrases tied to a single TV/social moment.
- Avoid `สู่ขิต`, `ตุย`, or `อยากตาย` in an office burnout pack because literal readings can create self-harm/review ambiguity.
- Avoid harsh blame (`ทำไมยังไม่เสร็จ`, `งานแค่นี้เอง`) and crude pronouns; the character should reduce workplace friction.

### Spelling, particles, and layout notes

- Use `ค่ะ` for statements and `คะ` only for questions; the selected pool contains statements and polite requests, so `ค่ะ/นะคะ` is intentional.
- Preserve `สู้ๆ` with the repetition mark and `แป๊บ` with the tone mark.
- Render Thai with Pango/HarfBuzz through the repository text-rendering workflow; do not use ImageMagick annotation for final Thai type.
- Leave extra vertical clearance for stacked vowels and tone marks even though wording is constrained to one line.
- Test the two longest entries—`เดี๋ยวอัปเดตให้นะคะ` and `เดี๋ยวส่งสรุปให้นะคะ`—first. Use condensed composition or a slightly smaller approved font size, never a second line.

## Handoff to create-line-stickers

- **Recommended sticker count:** 40 static stickers.
- **Character brief:** Original pear-shaped slate-blue office messenger pigeon with cream belly, coral beak/feet, two charcoal neck bars, one paper-tick crown feather, warm charcoal outlines, muted teal blank lanyard, simple generic office props, and broad work-to-burnout expressions.
- **Wording selection rule:** Use slots 01–36 exactly as the proposed single-line copy and 37–40 as wordless reactions; preserve the 24/8/4/4 job mix and do not add semantic synonyms.
- **Required invariants:** One written line per sticker; readable at chat size; original character and compositions; consistent female-polite register; no brands, personal data, copied ordered phrase list, profanity, gambling, substances, politics, religious solicitation, or self-harm reading; current slang limited to slots 33–36 and revalidated before artwork generation.
- **Open questions:** None required for first production. A later male-polite edition would need a full, consistent particle rewrite.
